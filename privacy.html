Auto Click and Slide Routine Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Auto Click and Slide Routine, also known as 오토 클릭·슬라이드 루틴, helps users run routines that they create and save themselves, such as clicks and slide gestures in selected apps.

Data We Access

Auto Click and Slide Routine uses Android Accessibility permission only to:

  • Check the currently active app package name and screen change events.
  • Run click and slide gestures that the user saved or manually started.

Auto Click and Slide Routine does not collect screen text, passwords, typed input, contacts, photos, files, or precise location through Accessibility permission.

Data Stored On Device

Auto Click and Slide Routine stores routine settings on the device, including:

  • Routine name.
  • Selected target app name and package name.
  • Click or slide coordinates saved as screen ratios.
  • Schedule settings.
  • Execution permission time granted after a rewarded ad.

This data is stored locally on the device.

Ads

Auto Click and Slide Routine uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and rewarded ads. Google may collect or process device and ad interaction data according to Google's advertising policies and privacy terms.

Data Sharing

Auto Click and Slide Routine does not sell or share routine data with third parties. Accessibility-derived information is not sent to external servers by Auto Click and Slide Routine.

Permissions

Auto Click and Slide Routine may request:

  • Accessibility permission: to run user-saved click and slide routines.
  • Display over other apps: to show the control widget and repeat-click marker.
  • Exact alarm permission: to start scheduled routines at the selected time.
  • System settings modification: to temporarily dim the screen during a running routine when enabled.
  • Notification permission: to show routine status and user-visible execution prompts.
  • Full-screen intent permission: to show an unlock prompt when a scheduled routine is due while the device is locked.
  • Foreground service permission: to keep recording controls, overlay controls, and routine execution visible while a user-started or user-scheduled routine is running.

User Control

Auto Click and Slide Routine runs only routines that the user creates, saves, and manually starts or schedules. Running routines show user-visible controls where supported, including pause, resume, and stop.

Children's Privacy

Auto Click and Slide Routine is not designed for children and is not intended to be targeted to children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email listed on the Google Play store page.
